It changes your cursor. That is all it does.

  • No administrator rights, no installer. Unzip and run.
  • No background process and no startup entry. The scheme still survives a restart.
  • No account and no telemetry. It goes online only for community previews and downloads you pick.
  • Changed your mind? Cursors.exe --restore puts every Windows default back.
